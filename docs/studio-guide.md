# Studio guide

Studio is the private page where Herman's team and staff run the site. Open `/studio` and enter the team access code. Every save shows on the island at the top of the screen: a spinner while it saves, then a check, "Live", or what to fix.

## Studio

| Section | What you do there |
|---|---|
| Timed notes | Add a note at a second in the song (Sound, Lyrics or Production), send it for review, approve it. Only approved notes reach fans. Editing an approved note sends it back to review. |
| Live update | Write a title and details, tick Approved, save. It appears in the Live card on the site. |
| Track details | Title, year, length, hook second, Spotify link, cover, release links, credits written as `Role: Name`, one per line. |
| Add a track | Gives the song its own page at `/t/<title>`. |

## The Pack

Open it with the **The Pack** button at the top of Studio.

| Section | What you do there |
|---|---|
| Sample data | Remove every sample wall note, item, giveaway and event in one click. |
| Orders | See every merch order. Confirm it, mark it delivered, or cancel it. Orders paid with Bachs show as **Paid online**. |
| Shows and meetups | Add an event with a photo, venue, city, date and time (Accra time), price shown, Bachs amount and capacity. Leave the price empty for a free meetup. Open or close bookings, confirm or cancel each booking, edit or delete the event. A show never sells more places than its capacity. |
| Name | What Herman calls his fans. It shows across the site. |
| Fan wall | Feature or hide notes fans send. Nothing appears until you feature it. |
| Giveaways | Create one with a prize and an end date, open or pause it, then draw a random winner. Entries stay private; the site shows a winner's first name and city only. |
| Shop | Upload a photo from your phone or computer (it is resized before upload), set the title, the price shown, the Bachs amount, the currency and sizes, tick "In the shop". Edit or delete any item later. |

## Listing a new piece of merch

1. Studio, The Pack, Shop, **New item**.
2. Upload photo.
3. Title, description, price shown (`GH₵ 250`), amount charged with Bachs (`250.00`), currency (`GHS`), sizes (`S, M, L, XL`).
4. Tick **In the shop** and press **Add to shop**.
5. Open `/pack` to see it live. Fans can now pay with Bachs or ask you to contact them.
